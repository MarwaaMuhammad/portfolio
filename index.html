About Me

I was a data analyst, working with numbers and patterns, but deep down I knew it wasn’t where my passion lived. The trigger came when I started exploring app development — the idea of building something people could interact with excited me. I took action by learning iOS development, but a major challenge hit: without a MacBook, I couldn’t fully pursue it.

Instead of giving up, I kept looking for another door. That’s when someone introduced me to the Android track — and it felt like everything clicked. I dove in, learned the tools, built projects, and found the perfect mix of creativity and problem-solving.

Now, I’m an Android developer who knows the value of adaptability, persistence, and turning challenges into stepping stones. My journey taught me that the right path isn’t always the first one you choose, but the one that makes you feel alive when you walk it.
